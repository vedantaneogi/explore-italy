The official links worth bookmarking

A curated directory of official tourism boards, transport operators, museum ticket offices, local-stay networks, food experiences, festivals and apps for planning and booking Italy.

Italy's travel web is crowded with resellers, lookalike ticket sites and inflated 'skip the line' offers, some of which charge double for a ticket you could book yourself in two minutes. This page collects the official sources: the national and regional tourism boards, the train, ferry and bus operators themselves, and the museums' own ticket offices, where prices are lowest and your booking is guaranteed.

Where an independent platform is genuinely the best tool, such as a ferry comparison site, a farm-stay portal or a home-cooking network, we include it and say so. Everything is grouped by what you are trying to do, from planning a route to booking a dinner. Links were checked in October 2026; if one has moved, the organisation's name is the best search term.

Good to know

  • Book museum tickets only on the official sites above; if a price looks high, you are probably on a reseller.
  • Download the Trenitalia, Italo and Moovit apps before you travel and set up your account on Wi-Fi.
  • Ferry operators release summer timetables in spring; book car spaces for July and August early.
